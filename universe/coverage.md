## Explicitly out of mandate

All sectors and all market caps are in scope (CLAUDE.md §2). Exclusions are
therefore properties of the *business*, not of its sector — named here so they
are not re-litigated:

- **No earnings power yet to compound:** clinical-stage biotech, pre-production
  mining, development-stage E&P, pre-deal SPACs.
- **Economics not tied to filings:** opaque holdcos, structures where
  unconsolidated JVs carry the majority of earnings.
- **Unverifiable numbers:** any thesis requiring trust in a figure with no
  filing behind it.

A name is never excluded for being small. Size is handled by the §2.1 liquidity
ceiling at sizing time, not by a universe rule.
