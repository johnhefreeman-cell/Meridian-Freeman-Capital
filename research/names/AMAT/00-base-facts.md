Unlike MRVL, AMAT looks like a **clean Type B (asset-heavy operating)**: it
manufactures capital equipment and carries a large installed-base service
business. Type B gate 1 — *≥50% repeat, or aftermarket attach ≥30% of gross
profit* — is the right test for semicap, not a forced one.

against it. If AGS clears the aftermarket test, the type taxonomy works here
