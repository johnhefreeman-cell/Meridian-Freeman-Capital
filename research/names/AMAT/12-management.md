# AMAT — Management Scorecard

Applied Materials, Inc. · CIK `0000006951` · **Type B (asset-heavy operating)**
Fiscal year ends late October; FY26 Q3 ran 2026-04-27 → 2026-07-26. All guidance
below is aligned to the **fiscal** quarter it was issued for, not the calendar quarter.
USD millions, one decimal. Scored 2026-09-05.

---

## Credibility grade: **C**

**Applied has never missed its own quarterly guide in twelve tries — but the guide is
engineered rather than forecast (12 of 12 landed above the midpoint, p = 1/4,096 if
unbiased), the one strategic claim management repeated to investors (market
outperformance) is contradicted by its own compensation scorecard in both FY24 and
FY25, and insiders sold $173.2M of stock in a nine-week window with the Rule 10b5-1
box unchecked on every single Form 4.**

The near-term number is reliable. Nothing else this team says forward-looking has a
verified base rate above coin-flip. Grade the two separately and do not let the first
buy the second.

`BEAT` = actual >2% above midpoint; `IN-LINE` = ±2%; `MISS` = >2% below.
No guide in this window was cut mid-year, so no beat below is scored against a
lowered bar. The Q4 FY25 guide (row 9) was a *sequential cut* — see the note.

the midpoint** — that is the operational conclusion from this table.

### The most damaging broken promise

**2025-02-13** — in the Q1 FY25 earnings release Dickerson told investors Applied was
"positioning Applied for continued growth and **outperformance** in the years to come."
Eleven months later the proxy disclosed that the compensation committee had scored the
"Grow wafer fabrication equipment market share (measured by VLSI Research)" objective
at **0.0 out of 1.0** for FY25 — a full miss, the worst score on the entire scorecard
— because share came in "below target for calendar year 2025" [DEF 14A 2026-01-28,
0001193125-26-027307, FY2025 corporate scorecard].

This is the second consecutive year the internal share objective missed (FY24 score
0.75, FY25 score 0.0) while the public language remained "outperformance." **The
shortfall was never disclosed in an earnings release.** It appears only in the proxy,
once a year, in a table nobody reads. That gap between the shareholder letter and the
compensation committee's own assessment is the single most damaging item in this file
and is precisely the class of finding the MRVL scorecard surfaced.

## 4. Alignment — compensation, ownership, trades

### 4.1 Comp metrics — CLAUDE.md §3.1 gate 5

**Verdict: gate 5 FAILS, twice over, and it is not close.**

CLAUDE.md §3.1: *"Comp tied to 'adjusted' anything is a gate 5 failure regardless of
type."* Every financial metric in Applied's executive plan is non-GAAP:

| Plan element | Metric | Non-GAAP? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bonus funding gate | Threshold **non-GAAP EPS** for the fiscal year | Yes |
| Scorecard, 10% | **Non-GAAP gross margin** targets | Yes |
| Scorecard, 10% | **Non-GAAP operating margin** + cash-flow margin | Yes |
| Scorecard, 2.5% | **Non-GAAP economic profit** (company-defined; uses a Bloomberg WACC) | Yes |
| PSU, 50% (FY23/FY24 grants) | Average **non-GAAP operating margin**, 3-yr | Yes |
| PSU, 50% (FY25 grants) | Cumulative **non-GAAP economic profit**, FY25–FY27 | Yes |
| PSU, 50% | Relative TSR vs. S&P 500, 3-yr | No — the only clean metric |

*Source: DEF 14A 2026-01-28 (0001193125-26-027307), 2025-01-22 (0001193125-25-010409),
2024-01-24 (0001193125-24-014287).*

Relative TSR is the only genuinely per-share, market-verified measure in the plan.
There is **no ROIC metric and no explicit per-share value metric** in the annual bonus.

The FY24 hurdle of $5.55 was set **below the prior year's actual of $8.05** — the
proxy states the committee "established a slightly lower threshold for fiscal 2024
compared to fiscal 2023, reflecting an anticipated less robust WFE market environment"
[DEF 14A 2025-01-22]. Meanwhile, at the time the FY25 hurdle of $6.25 was set
(Dec 2024), management had already publicly guided Q1 FY25 non-GAAP EPS of $2.29
[8-K 2024-11-14] — an annualized run-rate of ~$9.16. **The internal gate was set 32%
below the company's own public guidance annualized.** This is the same structure the
MRVL workup found: an internal bar set below the publicly guided midpoint. It is a
funding gate rather than a payout target, which softens it — but a gate that has never
been within 25% of the result is not a gate.

**Finding B — three-year PSU margin targets were set below the margin already
delivered, and paid above target for margin that declined.**

| PSU grant | Period | Threshold | **Target** | Max | Result | Payout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FY2022 (granted Dec 2021) | FY22–FY24 avg non-GAAP op margin | 26.4% | **28.4%** | 32.4% | 29.6% | **130%** |
| FY2023 (granted Dec 2022) | FY23–FY25 avg non-GAAP op margin | 27.0% | **29.0%** | 32.0% | 29.5% | **116.67%** |

*Source: DEF 14A 2025-01-22 (FY22 PSU payout table); DEF 14A 2026-01-28 (FY23 PSU
payout table).*

Applied's actual non-GAAP operating margin by year: **FY22 30.5%**, FY23 29.1%,
FY24 29.2%, FY25 30.2% [8-K 0000006951-23-000035 and 0001628280-25-051998].

The FY2023 PSU target of 29.0% was fixed in December 2022, when the company had just
reported **30.5%** for FY22. The three-year target was therefore **150 bps below the
margin already in hand**, and the 27.0% threshold — which still pays 50% of target —
was **350 bps below**. The delivered three-year average of 29.5% never once recovered
to the FY22 starting point, and it paid **116.67% of target**. The FY22 grant is the
same shape: target 28.4% against a 30.5% starting point, paid 130%.

Management was paid above target, twice, for a three-year period in which operating
margin went sideways-to-down from where it started. That is the sharpest item in the
proxy and it is exactly the class of thing the MRVL scorecard flagged.

**Finding C — the metric weight that changed, and the direction it changed.**

Relative-TSR weight in the CEO's annual scorecard, three years running:

| Fiscal year | Relative TSR weight (CEO) | Score awarded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FY23 | **8.0%** | 1.5 — "Outperformed peers during the fiscal year" |
| FY24 | **7.5%** | 1.5 — "Performed above target relative to peers" |
| FY25 | **5.0%** | **0.0** — "Performed below target relative to peers" |

*Source: DEF 14A 2024-01-24, 2025-01-22, 2026-01-28, FY23/FY24/FY25 corporate
scorecards.*

The weight on the only market-verified metric in the annual plan was cut **37.5%**
across three years. The 2.5 points removed for FY25 were reallocated to a newly created
**"Achieve non-GAAP Economic Profit goal"** objective — a company-defined measure that
scored **1.0** ("in line with target") in the same year TSR scored 0.0. Net effect of
the reweighting in FY25: 2.5 points of weight moved off a metric that paid zero onto a
metric that paid at target.

To be fair to the committee: the cut was decided at the start of FY25, when FY24 TSR
had just scored 1.5, so this was not a cut made *after* a known miss. But the shape —
weight migrating from a market-checked metric to a self-defined non-GAAP one, in the
year the market-checked metric collapsed — is the pattern, and it should be watched in
the January 2027 proxy.

**Finding D — gross-margin weight did NOT change.** It held at 10.0% of the CEO
scorecard in FY23, FY24 and FY25. The FY23 score was 0.5 ("below target"), FY24 1.0,
FY25 1.5. This is the one place AMAT is cleaner than MRVL: no weight was cut in the
year the metric missed. Recorded because the absence of the finding is itself evidence.

**Finding E — scorecard results are compressed into a narrow band regardless of the
business.** FY23 0.893–0.980; FY24 0.987–1.07; FY25 0.881–0.975 [DEF 14A 2024-01-24,
2025-01-22, 2026-01-28]. Across a year of record revenue (FY24) and a year with a
zeroed market-share objective and a zeroed TSR objective (FY25), the aggregate outcome
moved by ten points. The scorecard does not discriminate.

**Finding F — disclosure quality of the long-term goal degraded with the metric
change.** The FY22 and FY23 PSU operating-margin goals were disclosed as explicit
numeric levels (26.4/28.4/32.4 and 27.0/29.0/32.0). The FY25 PSU economic-profit goal
is disclosed only as threshold/target/max **percentages of shares**, with no dollar
goal [DEF 14A 2026-01-28]. FY25 economic profit was $4.2bn "in line with target," but
the target itself is not in the filing. Under CLAUDE.md §7 that goal level is
**UNVERIFIED** — we cannot tie it to a filing.

### Two dissents worth putting on the record before the kill is finalised

1. **The unchecked 10b5-1 box may be an artefact.** Applied's filing agent may simply
   not populate the affirmative-defence element even where a plan exists. The criterion
   as written turns on the plan being "disclosed at adoption," and it is not disclosed —
   so the criterion is met on the face of the filings, which is the standard CLAUDE.md
   §7 requires. But the honest characterisation is *"no plan disclosed,"* not
   *"no plan exists."* If the coordinating agent wants to contest the kill, the place
   to look is Applied's insider-trading policy exhibit to the FY25 10-K and any Item
   408(a) 10b5-1 adoption disclosures in the FY26 10-Qs. **This is the one open work
   item that could reverse the conclusion.**
2. **The June 2026 cluster has a benign mechanical explanation.** The 2026 proxy
   discloses that the December 2020 long-term Value Creation Awards to Dickerson and
   Raja "fully vested at the end of fiscal 2025" at a 200% payout modifier [DEF 14A
   2026-01-28]. A large vest followed by a large sale is ordinary diversification, and
   the two largest sellers are the two award recipients. What that explanation does
   *not* cover is the absence of any plan disclosure, the participation of the CFO, the
   CTO, the Controller and two directors in the same window, or the fact that the
   selling stopped within days of the stock's peak.
