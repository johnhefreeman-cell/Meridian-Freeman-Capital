## 1. Credibility grade: **C+**

Marvell has not missed a revenue or a non-GAAP number in twelve consecutive
quarters — but it sets the bar itself, it deleted the segment disclosure that
would let you check it in the same release as its largest-ever one-time gain,
and it is paid on metrics its own compensation committee defines.

Split the verdict, because the two halves do not average honestly:

| Dimension | Grade | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operating forecast accuracy | **A−** | Revenue 12/12, non-GAAP GM 12/12, non-GAAP opex 12/12, non-GAAP EPS 12/12, diluted shares 12/12 |
| Guide informativeness | **D** | Revenue landed above the midpoint in **16 of 16** releases pulled; non-GAAP EPS landed within $0.03 of the midpoint and never below, 12/12. A guide that cannot go down is a floor, not a forecast |
| GAAP forecast accuracy | **D** | GAAP EPS 4 misses / 12; GAAP opex 5 misses / 12 |
| Disclosure integrity | **D** | Five reported end markets collapsed to two, announced 2025-12-02 |
| Comp alignment | **F** | Gate 5 fail: annual bonus is 100% revenue + non-GAAP margin; LTI modifier is non-GAAP EPS and can only pay up |
| Insider alignment | **D** | Board + all executive officers own **0.124%**; $47.3M gross sold in trailing 6m against $0 bought |

Source: sections 2–8 below.

**Trend: improving on GAAP, unchanged on non-GAAP.** Misses across all eight
lines: **6** in Q3 FY24–Q2 FY25, **3** in Q3 FY25–Q2 FY26, **3** in Q3 FY26–Q2 FY27.
The improvement is real but the residual is now concentrated in one place — GAAP

acquisition special items, not from operating overspend. Non-GAAP opex was
+0.3% and +1.8% in the same two quarters.

### The finding that matters more than the hit rate

**Revenue landed above the guided midpoint in 16 consecutive releases** — every
Item 2.02 8-K pulled from 2023-03-02 through 2026-08-27, each of which states in
its own words that revenue was "above the mid-point of the Company's guidance."
Variance range: **+0.3% to +4.6%. Never once below.**

**Non-GAAP EPS landed within $0.03 of the guided midpoint in 12 of 12 quarters,
and never below it.** Distribution of (actual − midpoint): $0.00 ×2, $0.01 ×7,
$0.02 ×1, $0.03 ×1, plus $0.01 twice more. Against a stated ±$0.05 band.

That is not forecasting. That is a guide constructed to be cleared. It is
competently run and it is what CLAUDE.md §1 calls a noisy near-term print being
priced — except here the print is engineered smooth. **Underwriting consequence:
do not treat an MRVL beat as information.** The next-quarter guide, not the
beat, is the only forward-looking datum with content, and it has a systematic
+1% to +2% conservatism embedded in it that should be added back before it is
used in a model.

## 4. The Q3 FY26 quarter — what actually happened

Base facts flagged that **71% of FY26 GAAP net income ($1,901.3M of $2,670.1M)
landed in Q3 FY26**. Cause, confirmed:

> "On August 14, 2025, the Company completed the sale of its automotive ethernet
> business to Infineon Technologies AG for $2.5 billion in cash, which resulted
> in a **pre-tax gain on sale of $1.8 billion**."
> — 8-K Ex-99.1, 2025-12-02 `[0001835632-25-000193]`

**Management guided this gain and got it right.** The Q3 FY26 GAAP EPS guide of
**$2.03 +/- $0.05**, issued 2025-08-28 — two weeks after the deal closed — against
a Q2 FY26 actual of $0.22 and a Q4 FY26 guide of $0.36. Actual $2.20. That is a
BEAT of $0.17 on a guide that already carried the gain. On the guidance line
alone this quarter is a **credit** to management, not a debit: they told the
market the shape of the quarter in advance and delivered slightly better.

**But the same release carried the disclosure cut and the acquisition.** All
three in one press release, 2025-12-02:

1. the $1.8B gain crystallising 71% of the year's GAAP earnings;
2. "I am also excited to announce the acquisition of **Celestial AI** — a
   transformational milestone…" (CEO Murphy);
3. "Beginning in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, the Company will
   **consolidate** revenue previously reported separately as enterprise
   networking, carrier infrastructure, consumer and automotive/industrial end
   markets **into a new communications and other end market**."

**Framing did change at Q3 FY26 — but in the reported segments, not in the
guidance format.** See section 5.

## 5. Framework and disclosure changes

### 5.1 Guidance framework — UNCHANGED, and this is a genuine positive

Every one of the 16 releases pulled guides the identical nine lines: net
revenue, GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP gross margin, GAAP opex, non-GAAP opex,
basic weighted-average shares, diluted weighted-average shares, GAAP diluted
EPS, non-GAAP diluted EPS. Nothing added, nothing dropped, nothing withdrawn,
across 14 quarters and two CFOs. Marvell also guides GAAP EPS every quarter,
which most semiconductor peers do not.

### 5.2 Segment disclosure — CUT, 2025-12-02

| | Before (through Q3 FY26) | After (from Q4 FY26) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reported end markets | **Five**: data center, enterprise networking, carrier infrastructure, consumer, automotive/industrial | **Two**: data center, "communications and other" |
| Q3 FY26 detail available | DC $1,517.9 · ENet $237.2 · Carrier $167.8 · Consumer $116.6 · Auto/ind $35.0 | — |
| Q2 FY27 detail available | — | DC $2,171.5 (79.3% of revenue) · Comms & other $567.8 |

Source: 8-K Ex-99.1 2025-12-02 `[0001835632-25-000193]`; 8-K Ex-99.1 2026-08-27
`[0001835632-26-000022]`.

**A changed denominator is the oldest trick in the file (SKILL step 5).** Four
separately disclosed revenue lines became one. The company's stated reason is
the automotive ethernet divestiture, which is a legitimate reason to drop
*automotive/industrial* — it does not explain merging enterprise networking,
carrier infrastructure and consumer. The practical loss: the non-data-centre
recovery that management cited as a growth driver through FY26 ("a significant
increase in the pace of recovery in our enterprise networking and carrier
infrastructure end markets," 2025-08-28) can no longer be verified from the
filings. **Hand-off to `revenue-quality`: gate 1 (§3.1) now has to be scored
without the segment split it needs.**

### 5.3 EPS denominator changed mid-guidance-period — 2026-03-31

NVIDIA invested **$2.0 billion** in Marvell via Series A Convertible Preferred
Stock issued 2026-03-31 (8-K `0001193125-26-134462`, Items 3.02/5.03; Ex-99.1
"NVIDIA has invested $2 billion in Marvell"). Convertible into **21,778,000**
common shares; the proxy record date shows 875,553,173 common + 21,778,000
as-converted = 897,331,173 Voting Shares `[DEF 14A 0001104659-26-060253]`. From
Q1 FY27, "The Company calculated EPS under the two-class method as a result of
the issuance of the Series A Convertible Preferred Stock on March 31, 2026."
This landed **26 days after** the Q1 FY27 EPS guide was issued and is part —
though not most — of the Q1 FY27 GAAP EPS miss.

day. It is disciplined investor-relations practice and it has never yet been
used to disguise a cut. Note the timing: Durn was appointed **15 days after** the
Q1 FY27 GAAP EPS miss was reported.

### 8.1 Compensation — DEF 14A `0001104659-26-060253`, filed 2026-05-13

**Annual Incentive Plan (cash bonus), FY26 — three metrics, all of them the
wrong ones:**

regardless of type."* Two of three AIP metrics are explicitly non-GAAP, and the
proxy states the non-GAAP definitions "are determined by the ECC (in its sole

**The targets were set below what management publicly guided.**

Sources: DEF 14A `0001104659-26-060253` AIP goal tables; guided midpoints from
8-K Ex-99.1 2025-03-05, 2025-05-29, 2025-08-28, 2025-12-02; actuals from
`00-base-facts.md`. The H2 comparison is the sharper one: the bonus target was

**The weightings were changed in management's favour in the year gross margin
underperformed.** Non-GAAP GM paid **75.6% of target** in H2 FY26 — the only
sub-target metric of the year. The proxy is candid about why the weight was cut:

| | Actual weights (50/15/35) | FY25 weights (45/25/30) [EST] |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 FY26 payout | 148.9% | 144.3% |
| H2 FY26 payout | 140.8% | 134.0% |
| **FY26 Corporate Achievement** | **144.84%** | **139.2%** |

`[EST]` method: weight × "Percent of Target" per metric from the DEF 14A goal
tables, averaged across halves. The re-weighting added **≈5.7 percentage points**
of bonus. Small in dollars; diagnostic in intent — they cut the weight on the
metric they knew would miss.

**Long-term incentive — half right, half not.** PSUs vest on **relative TSR vs
the S&P 500** over three (and for one tranche five) years, capped at 100% of
target if absolute TSR is negative. That is a genuine per-share-value link and
it works: the FY23 grant paid **84%** on a 30.30% TSR against the index's 35.89%.
But the TSR payout is then multiplied by an **EPS Multiplier of up to 150% based
on non-GAAP EPS CAGR versus a 20-company peer group**, where "Non-GAAP adjusted
EPS will be based on the values disclosed in Marvell's… quarterly earnings press
release," and — decisively — "**In no circumstance will the EPS Multiplier result
in reducing the shares earned** under the relative total stockholder return
metric." A one-directional modifier on a number management defines in its own
press release is not a performance condition. It is a call option written by
shareholders.

The new CFO's package repeats it: 25,877 PSUs on relative TSR "further adjusted
by an **EPS multiplier of up to 150% based on the Company's Non-GAAP EPS**
compound annual growth rate relative to a peer group" `[8-K 0001193125-26-267688,
Item 5.02]`.

**Comp alignment verdict: FAIL (CLAUDE.md §3.1 gate 5).** Not because the
structure is unusual — it is standard large-cap semiconductor practice — but
because the house rule is absolute and every variable element here runs through
a non-GAAP number the company sets.

   that line is 0-for-8 on misses, 0-for-16 across every release pulled.
2. All three GAAP opex misses are **special-items** overruns, not operating
   overspend. In the same three quarters non-GAAP opex came in at +0.4%, +0.3%
   and +1.8% against guide — inside the ±2% band every time. Q3 FY25 was a
   $715.1M restructuring decision taken after the guide; Q1/Q2 FY27 are
   Celestial/XConn SBC, intangible amortisation and deal costs.
3. Counting the same economic event across GAAP GM, GAAP opex and GAAP EPS
   triple-counts one decision.

**But the count is 3, the framework is unchanged, and the literal text is met.**
This is a **borderline call, not a clear pass.** If the PM's reading is that any
guided line counts, this criterion is **TRIPPED** and the work stops. Flagging it
rather than burying it.

## 10. What to do with this

1. **Add back the sandbag.** MRVL's guided revenue midpoint has been beaten 16
   consecutive times by +0.3% to +4.6%, median ≈ +0.9%. Model the guide plus
   ~1%, not the guide. A "beat" is not news.
2. **Never underwrite off GAAP EPS guidance.** 4 misses in 12 on a line that is
   in-line 12/12 on a non-GAAP basis. Management forecasts the business well and
   forecasts its own accounting badly.
3. **Gate 5 (§3.1) scores FAIL** on the DEF 14A evidence, independent of any
   other gate. That is a full-diligence blocker under §3.1 ("must pass all six")
   and should be carried into the memo header, not litigated later.
4. **Gate 1 is now harder to score than base facts assumed.** The 2025-12-02
   segment consolidation removed the enterprise/carrier/consumer split. Whoever
   owns gate 1 needs to say explicitly whether it can still be scored PASS/FAIL
   or must go to **UNKNOWN**.
5. **§9.1 is a borderline kill.** Bring it to the PM before the workup proceeds.
