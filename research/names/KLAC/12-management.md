# KLAC — Management Scorecard

KLA Corporation · CIK `0000319201` · **Type B (asset-heavy operating)**
**Fiscal year ends 30 June.** FY2026 ran 2025-07-01 → 2026-06-30. Fiscal quarters
lead calendar quarters by two: 1Q26 = Jul–Sep 2025. Every guide below is aligned to
the **fiscal** quarter it was issued for. USD millions, one decimal. Scored 2026-09-05.

**Stock-split warning.** KLA effected a **ten-for-one split on 2026-06-11**
[8-K 2026-06-12, `0001193125-26-269375`; Ex-99.1 2026-07-28, `0000319201-26-000024`].
The 4Q26 guide was issued **pre-split** ($9.66 GAAP EPS); the 4Q26 actual was reported
**post-split** ($1.04). To score the pair I restate the actual to its pre-split
equivalent ($10.40). Comparing $1.04 against $9.66 would produce an 89% "miss" and is
the single easiest way to get this scorecard wrong.

---

## Credibility grade: **B−**

**The near-term number is the most reliable in the book — twelve consecutive revenue
prints above the guided midpoint, twelve consecutive non-GAAP EPS prints at or above
it, and the one multi-year public target this team set (June 2022 Investor Day:
$36.50–$39.50 non-GAAP EPS by CY2026) was actually delivered. What is not credible is
the compensation calibration: every EPS goal in the long-term plan was set 6.5%–9.4%
*below* the growth path KLA had publicly told investors was its plan, the
maximum-payout gate on the annual PRSU was reset to the 80th percentile in the same
month the committee certified 82nd-percentile performance, and there is not one
externally verifiable metric anywhere in the incentive design.**

Read the two halves separately. The guide is engineered rather than forecast — 12 of
12 landed above the midpoint, a 1-in-4,096 outcome if the guide were an unbiased
estimate — but it is engineered in the investor's favour, and it has never been
withdrawn or redefined. The proxy is where the value leaks: shareholders got on-plan
results and paid ~149% of target equity for them. That is a B−, not a C, because the
delivery is real and the framework is stable; it is not a B, because a board that sets
its own maximum bar below the number it has just certified is not calibrating, it is
accommodating.

### The most damaging item — 2024-03-18, and how it was described on 2024-04-25

This is not a broken promise about the business. It is a broken promise about the
guide, and then a description of the result that reverses the direction of the fact.

On **2024-01-25** KLA guided 3Q FY24 to GAAP gross margin **59.4% ± 1.0** and GAAP
diluted EPS **$4.93 ± $0.60** [Ex-99.1 `0000319201-24-000003`].

On **2024-03-18** — 52 days into an 89-day quarter — KLA filed an Item 7.01 8-K
revising that guide to GAAP gross margin **56.6% ± 1.0** and GAAP diluted EPS
**$4.06 ± $0.60** [`0001193125-24-070222`]. That is **−280bps** on margin and
**−17.6%** on EPS. Revenue guidance was expressly reaffirmed at $2.3bn, so the entire
revision is margin and charges: $60–70M of inventory write-offs and $50–70M of goodwill
and intangible impairment on the Display exit. The 8-K prints both columns side by side
— original and revised — which is to KLA's credit and is why this is checkable at all.

On **2024-04-25** the results release headlined:

> "GAAP diluted EPS attributable to KLA was $4.43 and non-GAAP diluted EPS attributable
> to KLA was $5.26, both of which were near the upper end of the respective **adjusted**
> guidance ranges"

and quoted the CEO:

> "KLA's March quarter results were **above our adjusted guidance**"
> [Ex-99.1 `0000319201-24-000009`]

Against the number investors were given in January, GAAP EPS came in **10.1% below
midpoint** and gross margin **152bps below**. Against the number substituted five weeks
before quarter-end, it was a beat. The word doing all the work is *adjusted*, and it is
the same word CLAUDE.md §3.1 makes an automatic gate-5 failure when it appears in a
comp metric. It appears here in the earnings headline.

**Why it is nonetheless not disqualifying:** the cut was filed on its own 8-K with the
original column shown, revenue was reaffirmed and delivered, the cause was a
$137M-of-revenue business (1.4% of the total) being exited, and it has not recurred in
the nine quarters since. One event, fully disclosed, not a pattern.

#### The three calibration findings — targets set against what investors were told

**(a) The maximum-payout bar was reset below performance already certified — in the
same month it was certified.**

In **August 2024** the Compensation and Talent Committee determined that the FY2022
annual PRSUs had been earned at **150% of target, at the 82nd percentile** of Relative
Free Cash Flow Margin. Those awards had threshold at the 30th, target at the 55th and
**maximum at the 75th** percentile [DEF 14A 2025, "Fiscal Year 2022 Annual PRSUs –
Performance Criteria Satisfaction Determination"].

In the **same month, August 2024**, the committee granted the FY2025 annual PRSUs with
these changes, in its own words: *"(1) we increased the maximum amount of shares
issuable … from 150% to 200% of target; (2) we increased the threshold necessary to
achieve the maximum number of shares upon vesting from 75% of the Relative Free Cash
Flow Margin to 80%"* [DEF 14A 2025].

| | FY2022 PRSU | FY2025 PRSU | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Threshold | 30th pctile → 25% | 30th pctile → 25% | unchanged |
| **Target** | **55th pctile → 100%** | **55th pctile → 100%** | **unchanged for 4 straight years** |
| **Maximum** | **75th pctile → 150%** | **80th pctile → 200%** | bar +5 pctile points, **payout +33%** |
| Just-certified actual | **82nd pctile** | — | **above the new maximum bar** |

The new 80th-percentile maximum gate sits **two percentile points below the 82nd
percentile the same committee had just certified**. The payout available at that gate
rose by a third. This is the AMAT pattern — a bar set below the number already
delivered — and it is sharper here because the certification and the reset happened in
the same month.

Note also the **30th-percentile threshold**: 25% of the target award vests for
bottom-third relative free cash flow performance against a peer group KLA selects.

investors.**

### The guidance-miss test, stated precisely — and the dissent

The criterion reads: *"missed its own guidance 3+ times in 8 quarters **and** has not
changed the guidance framework."* Both limbs matter, and the window definition decides
the answer.

- **Trailing 8 quarters (1Q25 → 4Q26): 1 GAAP EPS miss (2Q25).** On the live test, the
  criterion is **not met**. This is the reading I recommend and the one this scorecard
  adopts.
- **Historical window 1Q24 → 4Q25: 3 GAAP EPS misses** (2Q24 −22.7%, 3Q24 −10.1%
  against the original guide, 2Q25 −17.3%). If "in 8 quarters" is read as "in any
  8-quarter window in the record," the numeric limb **is** met, and the framework limb
  is also met because KLA has never changed its guidance framework (§3). **On that
  reading the criterion trips.**

**The dissent, on the record before the PM decides:** all three misses are non-cash
goodwill and intangible impairments of the PCB/Display business — $219.0M, $70.5M and
$239.1M — in a segment that was 5.6% of FY24 revenue and whose Display component was
1.4%, and which has since been exited. In each of those three quarters revenue landed
**above** the guided midpoint and non-GAAP EPS landed **at or above** it. The misses
are a balance-sheet event, not an operating one, and they stopped when the asset went.
Nine consecutive quarters have followed with no GAAP EPS miss.

**Recommendation: do not kill on this.** Log the historical window in the file so it is
not re-litigated, and re-test at each print. If a fourth GAAP EPS miss arrives inside
the trailing eight, the criterion trips on any reading and the work stops.

## 6. What this means for the memo

1. **The forward number is usable.** 47 of 48 metric-quarter pairs at or above the
   guided midpoint. When KLA guides revenue for next quarter, the base rate that it
   happens is 100% over three years, and the guide is a floor, not a centre. Underwrite
   the guide as a floor and model the beat.
2. **The forward narrative is thinner than it looks.** Six qualitative promises kept,
   none broken — but the two that matter most for the current thesis (the FPD exit
   completion date and the 2030 target model) were **never reported against and are not
   in any filing**. The 2030 model in particular must be marked **UNVERIFIED** anywhere
   it appears in the valuation.
3. **Gate 5 fails and cannot be argued around.** Insider ownership 0.0915%. Bonus tied
   to adjusted operating income in absolute dollars with no per-share link. Long-term
   incentive tied to non-GAAP EPS with a synthetic 14% tax rate. Zero externally
   verifiable metrics. This is a definitional failure under CLAUDE.md §3.1, not a
   judgment call.
4. **The comp calibration is the real finding, and it is worth a paragraph in the
   memo's variant-perception section.** KLA delivered its four-year public EPS promise
   to within 0.3% and will pay ~149% of target for it, because the internal goal was
   set 6.5% below the public promise and the maximum-payout bar was reset below
   performance already certified. That is a persistent, structural transfer from
   shareholders to management of roughly a third to a half of the long-term equity
   award. Size it in the dilution assumption; do not treat it as a governance footnote.
5. **The insider tape says nothing directional and should not be used as one.** $257.5M
   sold, $0 bought, 99.3% under disclosed plans. Under CLAUDE.md's own rule a 10b5-1
   sale is not a decision. But note what the absence says: not one officer or director
   bought a single share on the open market while the stock fell 40% from its high.
   That is not a sell signal. It is the absence of a buy signal, from the eight people
   who know the 2030 model is real.
